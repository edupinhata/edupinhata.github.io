## Introduction

I've been testing and realizing that how we start our mornings affects how the rest of our days, and even the next days will be.

If we neglect a good morning start, we may have a bad and unproductive day. Although, sometimes it's hard to wake up well and avoid all the bad habits that we have. Most of problems that I usually face during morning is: wake up really sleepy and try to wake up with tasks like look at phone, social media, videos, etc. When I see, I wake at 6:00 am, but I only leave the bed at 7:00, 7:30, and all this precious time is wasted.

This study is an initiative to have a more productive morning, and wake up better.
