# Introduction

In life, we have our days counted. Are we living like our days as they were counted? Like we had a specific amount of time to live in this earth and that's it? What's the legacy that I'l leave behind? At the end of my life, when I look back, what will I see?

How can I make my life different? How can I make other people life different?

We've been living in a time that everything is consuming. There is an huge amount of content that we can consume. More than we could in this life. If you consider movies, tv shows, books. Everything that humanity created is just too much. 

The question that many people try to answer is: how do we don't get sinked into all this. Also, there are studies that shows that these type of contents are made to hook us based on dopamine high doses. And what really matters at the end, is not completed.

I'm feeling addicted to all this content. Search for things that might make me feel entertained, not bored, accomplished. And the eager to consume more and more made me, many times, neglect my healthy. I'm not near as healthy as I could be. Actually, I'm not near as health as I needed to be.

Moreover, what I really need to make, keeps piling up. There is just an invisible wall that blocks me to accomplish it. And when the deadline is near, I get ansious, get sinked in my vices (food, porn). And this generate a cycle that is hard to break.
I want to break free from this. Live my life with meaningful actions. 

I was thinking about the groups that I lead. Sometimes I feel that I'm not being successful in all these tasks. Maybe this is what we're missing: something that make people to feel accomplished. With the feeling that they are progressing.

**How do we create this feeling?**

What I realize also, as I grow older, is that there are many things that I do, that I can't remember with clarity. I know that probably everything that I consume is part of me, which makes who I am, which relates to my personality, to my creativity. Although, many things I can't really remember and get nothing much useful from this. 

This is why I was thinking to live with more meaning: The idea is to do things and always take an action about it, so it become somehow meaningful:
- whatched a movie, serie? Take note of what was learned with this;
- read a book? Make a resume, talk about it.
- Created a though, document it. May it be a personal documentation, or a public documentation if this might help other people.

I believe that this will bring at least two benefits, and probably 1 problem.

## Benefits
1. Everything will be more meaningful. So instead of just watch, read, live something withough really think, get conclusions. I need to really think about what I choose to do.
2. Instead of just absorbing information and, when I pass away, what I've done is lost, I need to register this, so this might be useful to someone.
## Drawbacks
1. Do anything will consume a lot more time than just consuming it. So I'll be able to do less. Which means that I'll need to choose wiselly what to do, in order to don't waste time.

# Conclusion

1. Life is too short to waste it with things that will not make me feel accomplished.
2. Content sinking is a problem that I need to solve in my life, which is directly related to ansiousness and vices.
3. The culture of content absorption makes it hard to engage the people that I lead to the context that they are: work, church. How to make people feel more engaged?
4. I need to live my life producing and not only consuming. When I consume, I need to make sure that is somehting that will build me up. And if possible, create something productive to people.
