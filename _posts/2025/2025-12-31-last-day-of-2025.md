# My 2025 resume

And this is the last day of 2025. Day to reflect what has passed, and think what will happen.
Here are mine 2025 highlights:

1. I become a dad. My son/daughter will only be born by the beginning of July, but it's already growing in Esther womb. I will also become an uncle. 
2. I preached many sermons in church. With this I learned a lot about God, about how to preach. I still want to improve a lot my relationship with God and how I spread the Gospel. This will be a goal for 2026, become a man closer to God and to be an example to my son/daughter.
3. I kept developing myself in the company, mainly the last 4 Sprints were great, delivering features on time. Maybe I didn't work as effective as possible, so I feel that I had to work more. This is something that I want to improve next year.
4. I concluded two PHD subjects and entered in the PHD. In 2026 I need to focus more in the phd, not letting my work to fail.
5. I started swimming and got good at it. But I stopped cycling and was not as focussed in the gym as possible. I'm finishing 2025 with 105 kg, and my health will be one of the most important thing next year.

# What I want for my 2026

1. Read the bible consistently.
2. Read more books.
3. Organize my working life so I don't need to work overtime.
4. Read articles and publish papers for p.h.d.
5. Loose weight and participate of a Triathlon.


I was thinking to use a notebook to track what I need to do, but a notebook is not something that I take with me everywhere, so I'm thinking how to use my agenda in order to organize everything that I need to do.
